# Measurement and Validation

## Baseline

Record indexable/priority URLs; GSC clicks, impressions, CTR, position, queries, and pages; analytics organic visits and conversions/revenue/leads; Ahrefs estimated keywords/traffic, ranks, top pages, and links; crawl/index/schema health; Brand Radar signals; CMS/tracking state. Always record date windows, filters, source nature, and launch annotations.

## Day 7

Focus on deployment and discovery:

- compare approved objects/templates with the change plan;
- validate status, canonical, robots, sitemap, internal links, hreflang, and schema;
- check GSC discovery/indexing and inspect priority URLs;
- confirm analytics/conversion events still fire;
- crawl changed scope for regressions, 404s, loops, duplicates, and orphans;
- verify redirects and inventory lifecycle behavior;
- use Ahrefs/Brand Radar only as early directional signals.

Escalate material regressions immediately.

## Day 30

- compare GSC clicks/impressions/CTR/position distribution and query-page ownership with baseline and a comparable period;
- review new/lost/rising queries and pages by cluster;
- review organic conversions/revenue/leads with attribution caveats;
- compare Ahrefs rankings, estimated traffic, top pages, competitors, and links;
- review crawl/index coverage and unresolved issues;
- review AI mentions/citations/SOV with the same Brand Radar scope;
- classify positive, neutral/insufficient, mixed, or regression;
- decide keep, revise, consolidate, expand, or roll back.

Use day 60/90 for new domains, migrations, competitive markets, large programs, link acquisition, or slow recrawling.

## KPI cautions

| KPI | Source | Caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indexed priority URLs | GSC/direct | Coverage can lag/sample |
| Clicks/impressions/CTR/position | GSC | Averaging and privacy limits apply |
| Organic conversions/revenue/leads | Analytics/commerce/CRM | Attribution/consent can undercount |
| Keywords/rank/traffic | Ahrefs | Third-party estimates |
| Referring domains/backlinks | Ahrefs | Quantity is not quality or causality |
| AI SOV/mentions/citations | Brand Radar | Sampled prompts/models/window |

Each launch annotation includes batch ID, URLs, time, hypothesis, KPI, guardrails, observation window, confounders, and decision rule. Avoid causal claims from one before/after movement.
