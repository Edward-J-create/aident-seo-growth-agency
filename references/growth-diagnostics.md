# Growth Diagnosis and Report Revisions

Use for comprehensive growth programs, lead-generation businesses, multi-market research, or a revision of an earlier report. These modules supplement the existing keyword, architecture, technical, and measurement references; do not force lead funnels onto ecommerce or regulated review onto unrelated industries.

## 1. Business outcome baseline

Start with the supplied brand URL/information and competitor URLs. Independently inspect public offers, language versions, audiences, pages, CTAs and search signals. A complete public audit does not require private performance data or another report.

If an authorized first-party system is already available, optionally record the last comparable 90-day window: organic sessions, valid leads/orders, qualified opportunities/completed orders, revenue or value, audience, language, geography, sales cycle and invalid/abandoned reasons. Otherwise mark these fields as not measurable from public information and deliver the measurement specification. Do not take their values from a reference report or infer them from language pages, testimonials or third-party traffic estimates.

For lead businesses, define stages explicitly: action click, successful submission, valid/deduplicated lead, qualified lead, consultation/application complete, opportunity, contract. The business owner defines qualification; the analyst must not invent medical, financial or legal eligibility decisions.

Join page → event → CRM with an anonymous identifier when permitted. Do not send contact details, health information or free-text intake to analytics. Report first-touch and last-touch conventions, deduplication, consent limitations and pending cohorts. Never divide real leads by third-party estimated traffic and call the result conversion rate.

When data is missing, deliver a baseline collection table, not a fictitious funnel chart. Show the number of observations and unresolved cases before interpreting small-sample rates.

## 2. Competitor roles and market lanes

Use role × audience × market/language × dataset to choose peers. Distinguish organic scale benchmarks, local/direct competitors, business/cross-border competitors, and UX/trust references. A user-provided competitor may matter commercially despite weak organic visibility.

Discover peers using the workflow's own searches, Ahrefs Organic Competitors and current pages in addition to user-provided competitor URLs. A reference report can suggest a research candidate, but inclusion requires independent validation. Avoid one leaderboard mixing US estimates, Chinese demand, app subdomains and marketplace domains. Do not recompute a competitor median with different members and describe it as market growth.

For each competitor show: evidence; adapted strength; brand-specific prerequisite; what not to copy; proposed page/funnel owner. Service breadth is not automatically a content gap: confirm the brand offers the service.

## 3. Independent evidence and repeat-run comparison

For every metric store the originating page capture or tool result, retrieval date, observation window, target, endpoint, mode, market, filters, row cap/pagination, units and evidence nature. In a repeat run, use earlier workflow measurements only if the underlying retrieval is traceable; do not cite a prior narrative report as the source. Compare only equivalent scopes. Distinguish all sitemap assets from HTML pages, total traffic from Top Pages sample sums, live links from all-time links, and dofollow domains from manually assessed relevant domains.

Keep conflicts between independently retrieved datasets visible with a resolution step. Reference-report figures and diagnoses are not permitted as historical evidence in this workflow. Convert them into questions, independently investigate, and exclude any unverified claim from findings. Do not preserve a reference metric merely by adding “historical” or “unverified”.

## 4. Funnel and content coverage

Map awareness → consideration → trust → action → qualification → attribution. For each stage show existing assets, gap, unique page owner, CTA, event and business reviewer. Multiple sides of a market need distinct journeys. Adapt to store purchase, B2B RFQ, SaaS activation, enrollment or local appointment funnels.

For Chinese-language work, distinguish Google Chinese-language queries among overseas users from mainland search/social channels. Do not infer country, platform or demand from language alone. Select Baidu, WeChat, Xiaohongshu or other modules only when relevant to the authorized acquisition scope; native public platform research is not website SEO data.

## 5. Performance and public-resource boundaries

For full technical/conversion reviews, measure mobile and desktop separately. Distinguish CrUX/RUM field p75 from lab tests, single runs and historical measurements. Store device/network/location/cache, URL, timestamp, LCP element, transfer size by resource class and render-blocking costs. Use repeated comparable lab runs when warranted. A navigation-only lab test does not measure real INP.

Audit public app/portal/form/PDF/upload/staging surfaces when discovered. Record intended public visibility, status, index directives, sitemap/inlinks and authentication boundary. Do not collect personal records. A publicly indexed empty form is not proof of personal-data exposure. Robots blocking, noindex and authentication serve different purposes; noindex is not confidentiality protection.

## 6. Trust and link quality

For claim-heavy or YMYL sites maintain claim, URL, exact scope, evidence, reviewer, status, reviewed date and review trigger. Distinguish business, medical, legal, insurance and privacy responsibilities as appropriate. Verify competitor self-claims before adopting them.

For backlinks analyze follow attributes, topic relevance, destination owner, anchor context, new/lost state and duplication separately. Dofollow is not quality; DR is not a spam score. Mark unavailable dimensions explicitly. Link Intersect candidates need relevance and a genuine brand resource to justify outreach. Do not send outreach or automatically disavow domains as part of an audit.

## 7. Prioritization and release

Use Impact (1–5) × Confidence (0–1) ÷ relative Effort (1/2/3/5) as an editable analyst model, not an objective health score or promised ROI. Show components, dependencies, owner, acceptance condition and evidence status. Distinguish “P0 investigate” from a confirmed P0 defect; risk/dependencies can override numeric order.

Report revisions require a retained/added/corrected/unverified delta and preservation check. Validate 7-day implementation/discovery, 30-day query ownership and lead quality, 60/90-day cohorts, links and AI citations under the same scope. Do not infer causation from one before/after change.
